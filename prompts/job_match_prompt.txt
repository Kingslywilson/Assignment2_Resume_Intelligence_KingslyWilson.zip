Compare the extracted candidate profile with the analyzed Job Description requirements and identify skill and experience alignment.

INPUT:
Candidate Info: {candidate_info}
Candidate Skills: {candidate_skills}
Candidate Experience: {candidate_experience}
Job Description Requirements: {jd_analysis}

FORMAT INSTRUCTIONS:
{format_instructions}

RULES & CONSTRAINTS:
1. Compare candidate capabilities ONLY against evidence in the resume and requirements in the JD.
2. Identify:
   - matched_skills: Skills possessed by candidate that match JD required or preferred skills.
   - missing_skills: JD required/preferred skills NOT found in the candidate resume.
   - additional_relevant_skills: Candidate skills relevant to the tech domain but not explicitly listed in JD.
   - relevant_experience_match: Evaluation of candidate's experience against JD required experience.
   - project_alignment: Assessment of how candidate's projects align with JD responsibilities.
   - qualification_alignment: Assessment of how candidate's degree/education matches JD requirements.
   - technology_alignment: High-level technology stack alignment assessment.
3. NEVER assume undocumented candidate capabilities.
4. Output strictly in valid JSON adhering to the schema.
